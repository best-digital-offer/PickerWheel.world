# PickerWheel.world SEO Keyword Map

Research date: 2026-10-12
Target market: English-language users; prioritize US-style search intent while keeping the content globally useful.

## Research notes

- Search-result and competitor research surfaced wheel of names, spin the wheel, and wheel spinner as major demand terms. Third-party competitor reports show these head terms attract large search demand but face very strong competition; a new site should not assume it can win them quickly.
- Semrush keyword-level volume/KD retrieval was unavailable in the connected account because the account reported insufficient API units. This map records intent and a practical topic strategy, not invented volume or difficulty figures.
- Use Search Console queries and page performance after indexing to replace assumptions with the site's own impressions, clicks, CTR, and average position.
- The content strategy follows Google's current guidance: publish useful, people-first information; keep important content crawlable; use accurate page titles and descriptions, valid structured data, and a sitemap. No ranking or AI Overview appearance is guaranteed.

## On-page and technical SEO implemented

- Rebuilt the blog index around 15 visible article cards with clear topic summaries and internal links.
- Added a canonical URL, unique description, Open Graph/Twitter metadata, and Article JSON-LD to all 15 article pages.
- Added Blog and ItemList JSON-LD to the blog landing page.
- Added five new static articles with answer-first summaries, semantic headings, practical guidance, internal links to the tool and blog, and appropriate caveats.
- Rebuilt the XML sitemap with the homepage, blog index, all 15 article URLs, and existing informational pages.
- Updated robots.txt to point to the canonical sitemap at https://www.pickerwheel.world/sitemap.xml.
- Added llms.txt as a supplemental readable overview of the public site and its guides. This file is not a Google ranking requirement and does not guarantee AI visibility.

## Next measurement loop

1. Submit or resubmit https://www.pickerwheel.world/sitemap.xml in Google Search Console.
2. Inspect a sample of new URLs after deployment and request indexing where available.
3. After enough impressions accumulate, check Search Console queries and landing pages for each cluster.
4. Expand topics that earn relevant impressions and clicks; improve pages with impressions but weak CTR by testing clearer titles and descriptions.
5. Add genuine first-hand screenshots, classroom examples, testing notes, or original findings when available. Do not manufacture usage statistics or claim guaranteed randomness/security without verification.
