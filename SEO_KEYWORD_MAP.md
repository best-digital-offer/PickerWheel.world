## Existing-topic exclusions
Existing blog articles already cover: random name picker, yes/no wheel, number picker for games, classroom groups, classroom ideas, teams, color picker design, probability spinner, food/restaurant decisions, giveaways, randomness/fairness, privacy, and decision wheels. New articles therefore target narrower activity searches and should not repeat those articles' main intent.

## SEO implementation
New articles will have distinct title and description, canonical URL, Article/BlogPosting structured data, Open Graph and Twitter metadata, practical examples, FAQs, internal links to the live tool and related guides, and a direct-use CTA. The blog index, sitemap.xml, and llms.txt will be updated together.

## Quality guardrails
- No invented search volume, ranking claims, or guarantees of fairness.
- State how to prepare and clean entry lists, what each wheel is good for, classroom/game examples, accessibility and privacy considerations, and limitations.
- Do not target branded navigational phrases such as “picker wheel com” or irrelevant product terms that happen to include “picker”.
- Recheck Search Console after indexing to learn which long-tail queries actually earn impressions and clicks.
